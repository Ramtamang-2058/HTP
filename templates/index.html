Who We Are

Nepal's Oldest & Most Innovative Tech Company

Founded in 1984 by Muni Bahadur Sakya — Nepal's first computer scientist — High Tech Pioneer has spent over 40 years pushing the boundaries of technology in Nepal.

From demonstrating the first Nepali-language computing in 1983, to operating Nepal's first supercomputer in 2006, to now building humanoid robots and AI-powered mobile robots — we have always been at the forefront of Nepal's technological advancement.

Based in Dillibazar, Kathmandu, we are ISO 9001:2015 certified and committed to connecting Nepal to the global digital economy through cutting-edge hardware, software, artificial intelligence, and robotics solutions.

Our History

40+ Years of Pioneering

From displaying the first Nepali text on a computer to building humanoid robots — every decade marks a new frontier in Nepal's technological history.

1983

First Devanagari Computing in Nepal

Muni Bahadur Sakya demonstrated the first-ever Nepali-language display on a CP/M-based microcomputer, showing the National Anthem in Devanagari script. A historic moment that opened the door to Nepali computing. Awarded the Science Award in December 1983 for this achievement.

1984 – 1985

High Tech Pioneer Founded

Established High Tech Pioneer Pvt. Ltd. at Dillibazar, Kathmandu — Nepal's first commercial computer hardware company. Began manufacturing computer components and accessories, laying the foundation for Nepal's domestic technology industry.

1995

Sun Moon Computer Industry — Nepal's First Computer Manufacturer

Opened Sun Moon Computer Industry, Nepal's first company for manufacturing computer cards commercially. Took hardware production to a fully commercial scale, reducing Nepal's dependency on imported computer components.

2005

RONAST Award for Science & Technology

Received the prestigious Royal Nepal Academy of Science & Technology (RONAST) award for outstanding and sustained contributions to science and technology development in Nepal.

May 17, 2006

Nepal's First Supercomputer Demonstrated

For the first time in Nepal, a supercomputer with 16 nodes was publicly demonstrated. The system ran on open-source OS with OpenMosix and Oscar, using sixteen computers in a cluster configuration — a national milestone for high-performance computing in Nepal.

2016

ICT Pioneer Award

Muni Bahadur Sakya received the ICT Pioneer Award in recognition of his lifetime contributions to Nepal's information and communications technology sector and his role in shaping the nation's digital landscape.

2020s – Present

Robotics, AI & The Next Era

Now actively building InMoov humanoid robots, AI-powered wheeled robots (Jetson Nano + YOLO + OpenAI VLM + Whisper + DeepFace), Smart Mirror systems, and advanced Devanagari NLP tools. High Tech Pioneer is leading Nepal's entry into the global AI and robotics age.

Muni Bahadur Sakya

  Patan, Kathmandu · Est. 1984

Biography

Born in 1942 in Patan, Lalitpur, Muni Bahadur Sakya is the founding father of computing in Nepal. In December 1983, he made history by displaying the Nepali language on a CP/M microcomputer for the very first time — receiving the Science Award for this groundbreaking achievement. He went on to found High Tech Pioneer Pvt. Ltd. in 1984, Nepal's first commercial computer hardware company, and Sun Moon Computer Industry in 1995, Nepal's first computer manufacturer.

In 2006, he publicly demonstrated Nepal's first supercomputer — a 16-node cluster running on open-source OS — a national milestone. Today he leads High Tech Pioneer's charge into humanoid robotics, AI, and Devanagari language computing, placing Nepal firmly on the global technology map.
