The founder

Muni Bahadur Sakya

Born in Patan in 1942. Radio engineering in Calcutta (1962), computer engineering in England (1970), mainframes in France — then home, to build.

He assembled Nepal's first microcomputer in 1979 from parts gathered across three continents. In December 1983 his CP/M machine displayed the national anthem in Devanagari script — the first computer to read Nepali — earning a national Science Award. He founded High Tech Pioneer in 1984, opened Nepal's first computer-card factory in 1995, built a Nepali-speaking robot in 2005 and demonstrated the country's first supercomputer in May 2006.

Honoured by RONAST (2005), the CAN Federation ICT Business Excellence Award (2012) and the ICT Pioneer Award (2016). His work is documented by Wikipedia, The Record and CAN Federation.
